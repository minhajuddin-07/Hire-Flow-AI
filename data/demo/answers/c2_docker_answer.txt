At Veloce Commerce, although our central devops team managed the root Kubernetes cluster, I personally authored and maintained the multi-stage production Dockerfiles for our four FastAPI microservices. I configured our GitLab CI/CD runner to build, run vulnerability scans with Trivy, and push tagged container images to AWS ECR. When containers crashed under memory pressure in production, I debugged OOM errors, configured healthchecks and resource limits, and rolled out blue/green container updates with zero customer downtime.
