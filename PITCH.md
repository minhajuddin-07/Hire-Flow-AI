# HireFlow Pitch Versions

---

## 30-Second Elevator Pitch

> *"Resumes can claim anything, but they rarely prove production competence. Traditional AI tools try to replace recruiters with opaque match scores and automated rejections.  
> 
> **HireFlow** is an evidence-driven interview copilot, not a resume screener. It finds what a resume can't prove, suggests high-yield questions for the human interviewer, updates requirement evidence dynamically when notes are pasted, and explains every conclusion with verified verbatim quotes.  
> 
> **The AI surfaces evidence; humans make the decision.**"*

---

## 60-Second Extended Pitch

> *"Every year, companies miss out on top engineering talent because keyword screeners reject non-traditional resumes, while interviewers rely on subjective gut feeling.  
> 
> **HireFlow** transforms recruitment from subjective guessing into auditable, evidence-driven pair programming between recruiter and AI.  
> 
> Here is how it works:
> 1. **Zero Hallucinations**: Every single status claim is tied to a verbatim quote verified in source code. If an LLM invents a claim, our code rejects it.
> 2. **Targeted Copilot Guidance**: When a candidate has partial evidence—like local Docker dev without CI/CD proof—HireFlow generates the exact question needed to probe deeper.
> 3. **The Hero Loop**: The interviewer types or pastes the candidate's answer into the Interview Room, and in under 5 seconds, requirement statuses update from Partial to Clear, complete with before/after diffs and audit logs.
> 4. **Strict Fairness**: No numeric rankings, no declared winners, and no hire/reject decisions. PII and colleges are stripped prior to mapping, and our Bias-Safe mode protects screening.
> 
> **HireFlow finds what a resume can't prove and proves what an interview discovers.**"*
